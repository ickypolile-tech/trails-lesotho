About Lesotho

Discover the people, culture and beauty of the Mountain Kingdom.

Welcome to Lesotho

Lesotho is a mountainous country surrounded by South Africa. It is known for its beautiful highlands, rivers, waterfalls and welcoming communities.

Maseru is the capital city. Basotho culture includes traditional music, crafts, the Basotho blanket and the mokorotlo hat.

Visitors can explore historic places, experience local traditions and enjoy the natural beauty of the country.

Why Visit Lesotho?

Beautiful Mountains

Discover high mountain ranges, valleys, rivers and waterfalls.

Basotho Culture

Learn about local traditions, history, crafts and ways of life.

Adventure

Enjoy pony trekking, hiking, scenic drives and photography.
